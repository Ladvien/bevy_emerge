## Bugs fixed
### #196: undo may be refused for a yaw whose f32 has many digits
Must-fix: blocks no, common no (a fractional yaw from an agent, or a turn step that is not a whole number of degrees), no workaround no (a refused undo of the person's or agent's own change cannot be taken back from the editor) — filed as a bug and the oldest claimable one, so taken first.
Root cause (measured, not inferred): 484 of the 3599 yaws from 0.1 to 359.9 in 0.1 steps fail the JSON text round trip under `serde_json` 1.0.152's default parser (3.9, 9.1, 9.4, 10.4, ...), as do 0.03 and 0.11; the issue's example 12.3 does not. With `float_roundtrip` the same scan finds 0. The settings change's origin and enclosure wish fail it too, so the bug was wider than yaw.
Regression check: `tests/an_f32_in_the_log_still_undoes.rs` (2 tests). Failed before at the base (a65eb89 + the test): `undo of a turn to 3.9 was refused: ... (yaw is 3.9000000953674316 and 0000-1552 left 3.900000095367432)` and `undo of the settings was refused: ... enclosure_wish is 0.23000000417232513 and 0000-1552 left 0.2300000041723251`. Passes after: 18 passed with the lift and turn tests, `placement_set_lift`'s `lift_json` removed.
