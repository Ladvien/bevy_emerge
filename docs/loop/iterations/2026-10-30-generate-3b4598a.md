## Enhancement: a solve inside an area
Goal: a generate can fill one region and leave the rest of the map as it is. Files: `crates/bevy_emerge_core/src/grammar.rs`, `crates/bevy_emerge_core/tests/a_generate_lays_only_inside_its_area.rs`.
The solver works on the area plus a one-cell ring cut to the map. Ring cells beside the area are pinned to what stands in them (first placement, else a stamp, as the grammar's prototype; empty when nothing), left open when the grammar does not know the piece; the ring's corners are left open. The ring is never laid. An area with no cell of the map is refused, naming it. `solve`/`solve_constrained` are the same code with the whole map as the area.
Proof: the test file did not compile before (no `solve_in`). Mutation checks: pinning the ring open fails `the_area_joins_the_pieces_around_it` at seed 0 (the hole gets the other colouring); pinning corners fails `a_corner_of_the_ring_touches_nothing_it_lays` ("no arrangement satisfies what you have pinned").
