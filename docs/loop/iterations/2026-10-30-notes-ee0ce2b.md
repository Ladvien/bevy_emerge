## Enhancement: the note box's text stays on screen (#177)
Goal: a person typing a note sees what they type, even when the Inspector is scrolled down to NOTES. · Files: `crates/bevy_emerge_mapper/src/editor.rs` (`keep_note_box_on_screen`, `InspectorBodyQuery`, schedule, comment at `MapControlsPane`), `tests/the_note_box_is_on_screen_while_typed.rs`, `tests/all.rs` (one `mod` line, owner exception). · Proof: `the_note_box_is_on_screen_while_typed::typed_text_is_inside_the_inspector_scrolled_to_its_bottom` uses a 1280x720 surface. It scrolls the Inspector to its bottom (the precondition asserts that LAST is then off the pane), opens the box and types 'abc'. Before the fix it failed: "the typed note … is at (-348.0, -316.0), outside the Inspector's visible (135.0, 658.0)". After the fix it passes.
Design: `chrome::Follow` is keyed on `(text.len(), auto, pieces.len())`. The follower re-arms when the box opens and on every key, Tab or Shift-click, and never on a wheel. A person who scrolls back to NOTES therefore stays there until the next key. Assumed decision: of the issue's three options (scroll LAST into view, draw the text in the viewport, or show it in the band), I took the first because it is the smallest. It reuses the follower pattern every list already uses, and the other two would add a second place that shows the draft.
MCP: none. The change is a view only, so it needs no operation and no undo.
