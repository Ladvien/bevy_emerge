## Enhancement: Inside (#183)
Goal: a piece stays inside an area of another (a table on the rug, a chest inside the room's floor, a stall in the market square); `Near` is a circle, so around a long hall's centre it spills out of the sides or misses the ends.
Research (home-still): Merrell et al. (`furnitureLayout2`) penalise each piece's overlap with the complement of the room polygon; ProcTHOR (arXiv 2206.06994, App. B.8.3) places each floor object inside a rectangle of the room's open floor plan.
Files: `bevy_emerge_core/src/relations.rs` (variant, parse, check, propose, solve doc), `bevy_emerge_mapper/src/editor.rs` (RELATIONS row `table inside rug`), `docs/guide/agents.md`.
Proof: `bevy_emerge_core/tests/a_piece_inside_an_area.rs`, 6 tests. Before (this clone at fb2f57d with the test added): `error[E0599]: no variant named Inside found for enum bevy_emerge_core::relations::Kind`. After: 6/6 pass; clippy `-D warnings` clean on core.
MCP: no new tool or operation; `relations_solve` (undoable as before) proposes the move.
Assumed decision (on #183): the area is a rectangle given in the relation (`width`, `depth`) in the other's frame, not the other's descriptor footprint (relations read no library, and a room's walkable floor is not its mesh bounds) and not a polygon (one rectangle covers rugs, rooms and squares; a polygon can come later as its own kind).
