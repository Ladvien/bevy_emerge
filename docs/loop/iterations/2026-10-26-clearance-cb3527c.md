## Summary

`Descriptor::clearance` — *"space that must stay free around the piece"* since the schema was written —
was read by nothing that decides space. `stack::blocked_clearances` now reads it: each `Clearance` is a
band lying outside the keeper's own footprint on the side it names, and any placement reaching into that
band at a height their spans share is a finding. `checks` carries it as `clearances_in_the_way` beside
`doorways_in_the_way`, so every document-changing operation answers it. E-079 is now complete.

## Enhancement: a piece standing in reserved space is reported

Goal: Merrell's "0.91 m around this dining table" is honoured by something that decides space.

- **Crosses the layers, and has to.** Same reason `blocked_openings` exists: `same_layer` is `false` for
  every heterogeneous pair (a wall and a floor piece meeting is how a room is built), so a wall inside a
  table's pull-out space is not — and cannot be — an overlap. The question is asked about the *band*.
- **Measured from the edge.** Direction is world `yaw + align.front`; the footprint's extent along it is
  the exact projection `half.0·|a0·u| + half.1·|a1·u|`, so a piece turned 45° reserves from its own edge,
  not from a circumscribed square. `Around` is four bands widened across so the corners are covered.
- **Tutenel et al. (2010)'s exemption**, quoted in `Clearance`'s own doc, is implemented: an intruder
  that reserves space itself may share the band, so a dining set's chairs do not flag each other. Also
  exempt: `Tiled` (a rug under the table), `Decal`, a rider on the keeper, a piece whose span misses the
  keeper's.
- **The vertical bound is stated, not derived.** A sconce at 1.8 m over a 0.76 m table is clear. Head
  height is a number nothing in the schema carries, so this is written into the doc rather than guessed.
- **`stack::bodies()` is shared** with `blocked_openings` — `resolve_y` once, then each placement's plan
  box, span and front — so the two checks cannot disagree about where a piece is.

Files: `crates/bevy_emerge_core/src/stack.rs` (`blocked_clearances`, `clearance_band`, `bodies`, `Body`),
`crates/bevy_emerge_mapper/src/checks.rs` (`Found::clearances`, `find`, `report`).

Proof: `crates/bevy_emerge_core/tests/a_piece_in_reserved_space_is_reported.rs` — 8 tests, green, and

"intruder": "crate@2"}], "new_total": 1, "total": 1}`; a crate at 2.6 m out answers `new_total: 0,
total: 1`.
